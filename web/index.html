Arm calibration
Folded pieces on both grippers, marker 11 on the wall 50 cm ahead. About 35–40 min.
Tip anchors
Touch a point exactly and save it; 3–4 at different spots, then refit.
Tool weight (gravity compensation)
Nothing on the cup, arm at pre-pick. Weigh tool: 16 still postures, the motors' torques give the real mass after joint 7 (~3 min). Apply weight writes it into the URDF; then restart run_launch_everything.sh.
Automatic calibration (marker box)
Print the box sheets (glue on a 170×100×25 box) and the board at 100 %. 1: hold the board in view, Capture, move it, 3–5 times, Fit. 2: cup on the cross of face B, arm at pre-pick, Cal tip (~15 min, unattended).
Camera tip check
Measures where the camera sees the tool's tip, against the arm model. Every touch does this by itself before going in; this shows it on the picture (cyan: the tool's end, green: tip seen, orange: tip by the model).
Camera pose
After the camera has moved. Apply writes the result.
