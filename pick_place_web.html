Drag to reorder, × to remove. The saved order is what the next cycle runs.

The faded steps are the approach and are not reorderable: the pre-flight proves the whole descent column from the staging pose as one unit and picks the arm configuration that makes it flyable, so changing what it depends on would not give a different cycle, it would give an unchecked one.

An order that cannot work — a grasp before the descent, a release holding nothing — is refused with the reason rather than saved.
